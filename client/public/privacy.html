Privacy Policy for Gadhaa (Ace)

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gadhaa (Ace) game application available on the web and on mobile devices via Google Play. We value your privacy and believe in complete transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Gadhaa is designed to be privacy-first and does not require account creation, email registration, phone numbers, or passwords:

  • Display Name & Avatar: When joining a room, you select a temporary screen name and avatar. This is only stored temporarily in room memory for gameplay and locally in your browser storage.
  • Game Statistics: Win/loss records and donkey counts are saved locally on your device (in LocalStorage).

2. Microphone Access & Voice Chat (WebRTC)

Our application includes an optional real-time peer-to-peer voice chat feature.

  • Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Audio is transmitted directly between participating devices over encrypted WebRTC (SRTP/DTLS).
  • Zero Server Recording or Storage: Voice audio is never recorded, never saved, never transcribed, and never stored on our servers or with any third party.
  • On-Demand Permission: Microphone permission is only requested when you explicitly tap the "Join Voice" button. You can mute or leave voice chat at any time.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use advertising trackers, cross-site trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics. Standard session tokens are used solely to let you reconnect to your active card game table if your connection drops.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Security

All game state communication occurs over secure HTTPS and WSS (WebSocket Secure) connections.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub: https://github.com/Priyanshu9-bit/Ace
